About

Antimatter is an independent software studio. We build small, focused tools and take the time to do them well, rather than shipping something broad and unfinished.

Current Product

Sundew — a collection-tracking app for carnivorous plant growers.

  • Track genus, species, cultivar, and climate type per plant
  • Log watering and feeding, with dormancy-window tracking
  • Record substrate recipes and growing environments
  • Keep a photo timeline and a wishlist for plants you're after

What's Next

Sundew is our first release. Once it's out, we're moving on to more projects in the same spirit — useful, well-made, and built to last.
