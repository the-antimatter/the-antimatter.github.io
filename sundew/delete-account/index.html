Delete Your Sundew Account

Sundew is a carnivorous plant collection tracker made by Antimatter LLC. You can delete your Sundew account and its data at any time, with or without the app installed.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Sundew and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Type your account email to confirm, then tap Permanently Delete.

Your account and data are removed immediately.

Delete without the app

Sign in below with your Sundew email and password, then confirm. If you have forgotten your password, choose Forgot password? and we will email you a link that brings you back here, signed in, to finish deleting.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • your account (email address and password)
  • your plants, care logs, environments, substrate recipes, and wishlist items
  • your photos, including the stored image files

This cannot be undone. If you want to keep a copy of your collection, use Settings → Export first.

What is kept

  • Subscription records. If you ever subscribed, a record of the purchase is kept by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) and by Apple or Google, as required for billing, refunds, and tax. It is linked to an anonymous identifier, not to your plant data.
  • Crash reports. Crash reports do not include personally identifying information and are deleted automatically by our crash-reporting provider (Sentry) within 90 days.

Subscriptions are cancelled separately

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, or you will keep being charged.
