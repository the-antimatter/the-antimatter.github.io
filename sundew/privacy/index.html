Privacy Policy for Sundew

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Sundew ("the app") is a carnivorous plant collection tracker. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.

Sundew is operated by Antimatter LLC ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at antimatter@the-antimatter.com.

What we collect

Account information. When you create an account we store your email address and an encrypted form of your password. We never see or store your password in plain text.

Content you create. Everything you enter about your collection: plants (genus, species, cultivar, nickname, climate type, watering and feeding intervals, dormancy periods, acquisition details, price paid, vendor, notes), care logs, environments, substrate recipes, and wishlist items.

Photos. Any photos you attach to a plant. These are uploaded from your device's photo library and stored in our hosting provider's file storage. We request access to your photo library only when you choose to add a photo, and we only read the specific image you select.

Purchase information. If you subscribe, our payments provider records that a subscription is active for your account, along with its renewal status. We do not receive or store your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Apple or Google.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, we receive a technical report describing the error and where in the code it occurred. These reports are configured to exclude personally identifying information.

What we do not collect

  • We do not use advertising, ad networks, or ad identifiers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not use analytics that profile your behaviour.

Why we collect it

Your account information exists so you can sign in and so your collection is tied to you and no one else. Your content and photos exist because they are the function of the app. Purchase information exists so paid features unlock for the account that paid for them. Crash reports exist so we can find and fix defects.

We do not use any of it for advertising or profiling.

Who we share it with

We use the following service providers, who process data on our behalf:

Provider What they handle Purpose
Supabase Account credentials, your content, your photos Database, authentication, and file hosting
RevenueCat Subscription status, an app-generated user identifier Managing subscriptions and entitlements
Sentry Crash and error reports Diagnosing defects
Apple / Google Payment details Processing subscription payments

Each of these providers is bound by their own privacy and security obligations. We do not share your data with anyone else except where we are legally required to.

How your data is protected

Your content is stored in a database with row-level security policies that restrict every record to the account that created it. Photos are stored in per-account folders subject to the same restriction. Access to your data requires your authenticated session.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed as described below.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account from within the app at any time: Settings → Delete Account.

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, plants, care logs, photos, environments, substrate recipes, and wishlist items, including the underlying photo files. This cannot be undone.

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active subscription purchased through Apple or Google. Manage or cancel that through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to certain processing. The app provides direct access to all of your content, an export feature (Settings → Export), and account deletion. For anything else, contact us at antimatter@the-antimatter.com and we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Children

Sundew is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our service providers may process and store data in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for material changes, give notice in the app. Continuing to use Sundew after a change means you accept the updated policy.
